Jungle Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Your Privacy Matters

Jungle Media Downloader values your trust and is built from the ground up as a privacy-friendly interface. You can access all our core features entirely anonymously—we do not require sign-ups, email registration, or any personal user profiles to get started.

2. Processing Your Links Securely

Any URL or link you insert here is handled transiently to establish download streams via the underlying libraries. Jungle Media Downloader never logs, collects, or keeps records of your input history, search metadata, or media streams. As soon as your browser transfer wraps up, all relevant processing data clears right away.

3. Keeping the Servers Running (Cookies & Advertising)

Jungle Media Downloader doesn't deploy individual tracking software. However, maintaining high-speed open-source servers takes continuous work and resource expenses. To keep our service free for the community, we partner with third-party advertising networks to show supportive banner spaces.

These helpful ad networks may use standard cookies and anonymous web indicators to display contextually relevant suggestions. If you prefer, you can easily tweak or turn off personalized cookie preferences inside your local web browser settings anytime you want.

4. Moving to External Streams

Our online interface safely points to public media delivery URLs to compile files. Once a file starts downloading directly to your personal system space, the connection links with those external networks. We suggest looking at the friendly usage policies of those unique external platforms for clarity.

5. Updates and Harmony

As the internet evolves, we might update this layout page slightly to stay aligned with performance updates. Enjoying our free interface means you trust how we maintain this clean, zero-logs environment for you.
