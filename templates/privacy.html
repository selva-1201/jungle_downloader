1. Anonymity by Design

Jungle Media Downloader respects your privacy. You can access and use our service completely anonymously. We do not require account creation, email sign-ups, passwords, or personal demographic profiles to use any feature of the platform.

2. Transient Link Processing & No Storage

When you submit a URL for downloading, our servers resolve metadata and facilitate the media stream dynamically. We do not host, archive, or permanently store any user-requested media files on our infrastructure. Temporary tokens and format metadata stored in cache memory expire automatically after a short duration.

3. Server Logs & IP Processing

To enforce fair-use rate limits, protect against automated scrapers, and maintain service availability, our servers process technical request data including your IP address, requested URL, user-agent, and response status. Standard access logs are retained strictly for operational security and abuse prevention for up to 30 days, after which they are permanently deleted.

4. Cloudflare & Bot Mitigation

Our infrastructure is protected by Cloudflare and Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated attacks, credential abuse, and denial-of-service incidents. Cloudflare may evaluate technical browser telemetry and issue security clearance cookies to distinguish human visitors from automated bots without tracking personal identity.

6. External Media Hosts

When a download stream begins, your client connects to either our streaming proxy or directly to the target media provider's content distribution network. Those third-party hosts operate independently and have their own respective privacy policies and data collection terms.

7. Self-Hosted Typography & Zero Font Tracking

All typography and fonts (Space Grotesk) are self-hosted directly from our server. No client telemetry, IP addresses, or referrer headers are shared with Google Fonts or third-party font networks.
